The following examples are published with this guide. They are grouped by clinical scenario, so that the instances that reference each other (order, imaging study, observations, report) appear together. All examples are also available as a downloadable zip file (see Downloads).

Examples are non-normative: they illustrate how the profiles are intended to be used, but the formal requirements are in the profiles themselves (see Profiles). The data shown is fictitious.
